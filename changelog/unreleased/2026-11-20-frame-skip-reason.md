- **A skipped frame says why.** `FrameEncoded::skipped()` returns a `FrameSkip` for each `Skipped` report: `Covered` (the window isn't on screen), `Timeout` (no drawable came in time; a later frame tries again) or `Surface` (the surface needs reconfiguring or replacing before a frame can draw). `Drawn` and `Unchanged` reports return `None`. The reason is about that frame alone: an app can keep its state through an occluded or late frame, and a `Surface` skip tells it the window may not match what is on screen until a frame is `Drawn`.
