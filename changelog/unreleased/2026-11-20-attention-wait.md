- **Wayland attention can complete before a window closes.** With the new `App::attention_wait(limit)`, the event loop finishes a window's attention request (`WindowHandle::focus`, an `open` that focuses a shown window, a forwarded activation token) only once the compositor has processed it, at most `limit` after the request was sent. A `close` sent on the same `WindowHandle` after `focus` therefore can no longer drop it. A wait that runs out is logged once, and that window's later requests don't wait until the compositor answers again. Without it nothing changes.
